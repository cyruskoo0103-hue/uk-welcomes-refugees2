Our Vision & Mission

Growing Community-Led Sanctuary Across the UK

Formed in 2020, UK Welcomes Refugees (UKWR) develops innovative approaches to community-led welcome, resettlement, employment, and integration that bring reciprocal benefits to both newcomers and host communities.

We recruit, train, and equip local volunteers from diverse backgrounds to build lasting friendships, trust, and reciprocity—ensuring every person fleeing conflict or persecution is met with dignity, safety, and opportunity.

Safe & Legal Routes

Empowering Communities to Directly Sponsor Refugee Families

Community Sponsorship enables ordinary UK citizens to form local sponsorship groups, raise initial support funds, secure housing, and directly welcome refugee families fleeing war, persecution, and displacement.

UK Welcomes Refugees campaigns for transparent safe routes and provides hands-on guidance for lead sponsors, local councils, and community groups navigating the Home Office sponsorship approval framework.

How Community Sponsorship Works

  1. 1 Form a Group: Gather 5–10 committed local volunteers in your town or city.
  2. 2 Secure Housing & Support: Find suitable affordable housing and raise local starter funds.
  3. 3 Welcome & Resettle: Meet the family at the airport, assist with healthcare, English classes (ESOL), and school enrollment.

Local Integration & Volunteering

Equipping volunteers, fostering mutual friendships, and opening doors to education, housing, and social life.

Housing & Essentials

Working alongside local councils and housing providers to source stable, affordable accommodation for arriving families.

ESOL & Education Access

Connecting newcomers with conversational English clubs, formal ESOL classes, and supporting parents with school places.

Employment & Reciprocity

Mentoring newcomers to gain UK job qualifications and helping local workplaces tap into the diverse talents of refugees.

Honouring Our Founder

Neil Jameson CBE (1946–2023) • Pioneer of UK Community Organising

Neil Jameson CBE

Founder of UK Welcomes Refugees

"To be united with compassion towards each other—giving expression to our passion for a kinder and more just world."

Neil Jameson CBE was the founder of Citizens UK, TELCO, the Living Wage Foundation, and in 2020 launched UK Welcomes Refugees to promote community-led resettlement across the nation.

Having championed Safe Passage for unaccompanied minors following the Syrian crisis, Neil dedicated his final years to expanding Community Sponsorship so civil society could take an active role in welcoming displaced families.
